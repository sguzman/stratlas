# Stratlas public map gallery

The interactive [Stratlas map](https://sguzman.github.io/stratlas/) is the public-facing geographic gallery, with its source in [web/index.html](../web/index.html). The machine-readable [index.json](index.json) identifies documented existing demonstrations and links to their source and specimen records.

The Los Angeles entry describes an already implemented, **partial** geographic pilot. Its readable [specimen profile](../docs/metropolises/los-angeles.md) describes layers, source limits, current coverage and outstanding verification. Its inclusion here does not change or process the [specimen queue](../docs/queues/specimens.json), create downstream tasks, or certify metropolitan coverage. Individual layers and data sources retain their documented geographic boundaries, vintages, usage terms, limitations, and verification status.

Future metropolitan demonstrations may appear here only when publicly available and accurately described. Do not infer completion from a map rendering, registry entry, or queued specimen. Do not include external tiles, source response archives, private content, or assets lacking publication rights.
